About the role

Learn SEO, social media and paid ads on real client projects, guided by our marketing team — with a chance of a full-time role.

Freshers or final-year students with a strong interest in digital marketing and good communication skills.

What you'll do

  • Assist with keyword research, on-page SEO and content planning
  • Schedule social media posts and track engagement
  • Support ad campaign setup and reporting
  • Research competitors and industry trends

What we're looking for

  • Graduate or final-year student (any stream)
  • Genuine interest in digital marketing
  • Good written and spoken English
  • Willingness to learn fast

Nice to have

  • Google Digital Garage or similar certification
  • Personal social or blog projects

What you'll get

  • Real client projects and ownership of your work
  • Learning budget for courses and certifications
  • Mentorship from senior team members
  • Regular reviews and a clear growth path
  • Friendly team, celebrations and team lunches

What happens after you apply

  1. 01Day 1Apply online

    Send your details and CV through the form on the job page — it takes about 3 minutes.

  2. 02Day 2–3Quick call

    A friendly 15-minute call to get to know you and answer your questions.

  3. 03Day 3–5Skill round

    A short, practical task or portfolio walkthrough — no trick questions.

  4. 04Day 5–7Offer & onboarding

    Meet the team, get your offer and a clear plan for your first month.
